## P0: broken / presented as working but isn't

### P0-1 Elite Surge curse does nothing while exploring (waves on) - FAIL (code)
- What: the curse says "Random elites appear three times as often" and pays the highest gold bonus
  (+30%). With `Config.Waves.Enabled = true`, exploring never calls `topUp()` (it returns early), and
  the wave elite roll ignores the curse. The multiplier only reaches the small boss-fight crowd.
- Where: `src/server/Modules/EnemySpawner.lua:484-486` (topUp returns while waves are on),
  `:708` (`rng:NextNumber() < W.EliteChance`, no `RunModifiers.EliteChanceMult()`), `:522` (the
  only use of the multiplier). Same for the Daily Challenge whenever it rolls Elite Surge.
- Evidence: pacing-sim wave table: elites per wave are 0 / 1-3 exactly as `EliteChance` 0.5 /
  `EliteMax` 3 dictate; `curses-sim` only checks the multiplier value, not real spawns, so the
  regression suite cannot catch it.
- Fix: in `startWave`, use `math.min(1, W.EliteChance * RunModifiers.EliteChanceMult())` and let the
  curse raise the elite count (e.g. +1 elite per wave, EliteFromWave 2); or reword the curse.

## P1: bad experience

### Waves and in-run pacing

P1-1 Wave sizes are not "each harder" (owner spec) - FAIL (sim)
- Where: `EnemySpawner.lua:721` (`count = total / dirs * WaveCountMult[mainType]`), `:676`
  (pressure multiplier resets on the next stage).
- Evidence (pacing.log WAVE lines): S1 w5 21 < w4 22; S2 w11 42 < w10 50, w12 38, w14 41, w17 54 <
  w16 60; S3 w21 45 < w20 65, w23 46; S4 w31 73 < w30 104, w32 64; S5 w41 83 < w40 144, w44 87,
  w46 84. A side whose main type is Bat (x0.6) or Bomber / Brute (x0.5) shrinks the whole wave, and
  every stage restart drops the pressure x1.35.
- Fix: compute the wave total first and fill the shortfall of a halved side with the row mix (or
  Slimes); carry the pressure step into the next stage's first waves, or make the per-wave base
  monotonic (`max(previous size + 1, formula)`).

P1-2 Wave banner + horn every 12-18 s, elite toasts on top - FAIL (sim / code)
- Where: `src/client/StageUI.lua:678-712` (every WaveSeq change → `Hud.Announce` + `WaveHorn`),
  `EnemySpawner.lua:829` (one "An elite ... leads the wave!" toast per elite, up to 3 per wave).
- Evidence: 53 waves in a 21-minute run (10-13 per 150 s stage); from stage 3 about half the waves
  carry 3 elites = 3 toasts. The centre banner queue (`Hud.lua:566-575`, 3 deep) also delays the
  portal / pressure banners behind them, and a queued "WAVE N" can show after wave N+1 started.
- Fix: banner only for wave 1 of a stage, every BIG wave and the first wave with a new side count;
  other waves = pill + edge glow + a quieter horn. One toast per wave ("Wave 23 · 3 elites").
  Drop queued wave banners older than ~2 s.

P1-3 Level-up droughts in boss fights, then 5-6 cards in one second - FAIL (sim)
- Where: `EnemySpawner.lua:1063` (only wave members get `Config.Waves.XPMult` 1.8; the boss-fight
  crowd from `topUp` gets x1), `StageManager.lua:571-575` / `XPSystem.CollectAll` (every gem on the
  map flies in when the portal opens).
- Evidence (pacing.log, solo): first level-up 0:22 (good), then 13 gaps > 45 s (48-82 s, all in Boss
  / right after travel) and 12 bursts of 3+ levels inside 3 s ("4:16 S1 Open x5", "20:51 S5 Open
  x6", stage 3 "Opx2 Opx2 Op Opx2"). Stages 2-5 level only 2-3 times in 150 s of exploring; 60-75 %
  of each stage's levels arrive in the Open burst.
- Fix: apply `Waves.XPMult` to boss-phase minions too (they are the only XP source for 1-2 min);
  give the boss kill a fixed XP chunk; trickle the end-of-surge vacuum (e.g. collect over 3-4 s and
  cap queued level-ups per batch, rolling the rest into the next stage's first minute).

P1-4 Level-up card pool starves the starting weapon - ASSUMED (code + model)
- Where: `src/server/Modules/LevelUpSystem.lua:333-345`: every unowned "useful" passive gets the full
  `WeightNewPassive` 5 (no pool share), while new weapons are shared via `NewWeaponPoolRef`.
- Evidence (python model of buildPool weights, 20k draws, ~22 useful passives): first level-up:
  per-card odds PassiveNew 65 %, WeaponNew 29 %, WeaponUp 6 %; chance that ANY of the 3 cards
  upgrades the starting weapon 17 %. With 2 weapons / 2 passives: 31 %. With 26 passives released
  (11 new ones in this update) early runs mostly fill passive slots.
- Fix: share the new-passive weight like weapons (`NewPassivePoolRef` ~8), or guarantee one weapon
  card in the first N offers.

P1-5 Ward Shields far above every other area weapon - ASSUMED (data + code)
- Where: `src/shared/WeaponData.lua:885-903`, behaviour `WeaponSystem.lua:1660-1720`.
- Evidence: each shield hits every enemy it passes once per orbit (Rehit 0.55 s < orbit period
  ~1.2 s). Level 12: 5 shields x 28 dmg / 1.23 s = ~114 dmg/s to EVERY enemy in the ring; Aegis Ring
  6 x 50 / 1.24 s = ~240 dmg/s per enemy, plus reflect bursts. Comparable rings: Garlic L12 13 /
  Soul Eater 19 dmg/s per enemy; Holy Water evo ~22; Frost Nova evo ~22. Single-target index
  (damage x amount / cooldown) puts Aegis at 750 vs the next best 246 (Worldbreaker).
- Weak outliers (index at L12 / evo): Vortex 5.5 / 9.4 (2.5 base damage), Plague Censer 9.4 / 20,
  Vine Snare 15.7 / 31, Healing Totem 5.1 / 13 (support), Turret ~62 at L12 but ~11 at L1 for a
  7 s rebuild. Full table: `wf/audit/dps.txt`.
- Fix: Ward Shields damage x0.5 and evo 30 dmg / 5 shields, or rehit per shield >= orbit period;
  raise Vortex / Censer base damage ~2x. Confirm with `weapons-sim` per weapon.

### Progression and economy (numbers in the last section)

P1-12 New players earn almost no permanent gold - ASSUMED (sim + config)
- Where: `Config.Gold` (`KillGoldChance` 0.12, `FailureRetainBase` 0.35), `Config.Chests.Cost`,
  `GoldSystem.SpendRunGold` (chests are paid from the same escrow that becomes permanent gold).
- Evidence: pacing logs: kill gold before the stage-1 boss is only ~70-100 (n2-new: 97 gold at
  4:23); a stage-1 death keeps 35 % of that = 25-35 gold. Stage-1 loot costs ~600 gold (12 small x25
  + 2.5 large x60 + golden 150) vs ~420-600 earned, so a player who opens chests brings nothing home.
  First hero upgrade costs 200, Mage 10,000.
- Fix (owner decisions, no prices changed here): pay kill gold to a separate run purse for chests, or
  refund unspent chest gold at 100 %; or raise retention on stage 1; or award a flat "run
  completed" gold (time-based) outside the escrow.

P1-13 Gold heroes cost the most, achievement heroes come almost free - ASSUMED
- Where: `CharacterData.lua:86/113/140` (10k / 20k / 30k), `AchievementData.lua` QueenSlayer (first
  Queen kill), Reaper (500 kills in one run), FieldEngineer (3 Bargain shrines, which are free and on
  every stage), DeepDelver (reach stage 4).
- Evidence: pacing-sim stage 1 alone has 398-428 kills; Ranger, Necromancer and Engineer normally
  unlock in runs 1-3, Alchemist with the first 4-stage run; Mage needs ~15 stage-1 clears without
  chests (or 2 four-stage runs), Priest (the simple starter-style hero) 45 / 6.
- Fix: make Engineer / Reaper goals real (e.g. 3 Guarded Altars, 1,500 kills) or lower gold prices;
  at least show "Earned by playing" vs "Gold" grouping on the roster.

P1-14 Hero Mastery stat costs past level ~10 are unreachable - ASSUMED (data)
- Where: `src/shared/MetaUpgradeData.lua:33-55` (MaxHP 200 x1.5^n, Might 300 x1.5^n, 20 levels),
  now bought per hero (x8).
- Evidence: MaxHP level 20 alone costs 443,368 gold, all 20 levels 1.33 M; Might 2.0 M. All upgrades
  to cap: mastery 1 = 5,100, mastery 2 = 19,030, mastery 3 = 55,006, mastery 5 = 256,642, mastery 10 =
  3,530,839 gold per hero (x8 heroes ~28 M). A perfect invincible 5-stage run earns ~9.4-10.2 k. The
  mastery cap stops mattering after mastery 2-3; gold is the only wall.
- Fix: flatten growth for per-hero stats (e.g. 1.15-1.2, or cap MaxHP / Might at 10 levels), so
  mastery levels actually unlock something buyable.

## Progress too slow? (numbers)

Run pace (pacing-sim, solo Knight, invincible, AI walking a circle, waves on):
- First level-up 0:22; stage 1 = 14 levels (6 while exploring), stages 2-5 = 11-12 levels each but
  only 2-3 of them while exploring; 13 gaps > 45 s, 12 bursts of 3+ cards in 3 s.
- Damage taken per minute vs max HP: stage 1 explore 135 / 144 HP, stage 2 explore 451 / 144,
  stage 3 boss 940 / 168, stage 4 boss 1,689 / 240: stage 2 is where an average player dies.
- Run gold earned (no chests bought): stage 1 +594, stage 2 +937, stage 3 +1,394, stage 4 +2,084,
  stage 5 +4,356 (total 9,365; the 5 boss kills are 1,000 of it).
